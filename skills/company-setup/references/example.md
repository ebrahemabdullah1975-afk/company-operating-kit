# Worked example (anonymised)

A pre-scale UK food start-up making chilled, microwave-heated toasties ran itself this way. Names of suppliers, people and documents are removed, and the figures below are illustrative of the shape of entries, not data to reuse.

## Setup choices
- GM plus eight departments: R&D, Procurement, Operations, Quality, IT/AI, Marketing, Commercial & sales, Finance. IT/AI was added a day after launch to own the automations.
- Five validation phases before any capital spend: benchtop recipe test, street pop-up, store pilot, lab and economics, commercial pitch. Each had one measurable target and a budget.
- Dispatcher daily at 06:32; weekly review Mondays at 07:37.

## What good entries looked like

**Key assumption (planning, not a target):**
"Fully loaded unit cost: £1.80 is a planning figure from the cost model, not yet verified. No cost target is fixed until supplier quotes arrive (GM decision, <date>)."

**Decision row:**
| Date | Decision | Departments affected | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| <date> | Define Phase 1 "crisp" as a panel score of 4 or more on a 1 to 5 scale, 10 minutes after heating. | R&D, Quality | The brief had no measure for the Phase 1 target. A sensory panel fits the Phase 1 budget, where an instrument test would cost more. Closes the open question on what counts as crisp. |

**Hand-off moving through statuses:**
Open → (dispatcher writes an RFQ) → "Waiting <date>: written supplier quotes for the sleeve (unit price, minimum order, tooling, delivery), to be obtained by Procurement" → Done once quotes are logged.

**Open question for the GM:**
"Is the Phase 2 budget gross spend or net of pop-up sales? As gross, stock alone caps the run at about 800 units. Options: gross, or net cash outlay. Owner: GM."

## Lessons from running it
- Planning figures drift into "targets" unless every line says what it is. Label each figure planning, verified or GM decision.
- An automation once recorded a decision the GM had not made. The fix was hard rules plus a before-and-after check of the Decisions log on every run.
- Withdraw unsourced statistics everywhere at once (brief, decks, content) and record the withdrawal as a decision.
- Real-world steps (supplier replies, kitchen tests, site permissions) set the pace. "Waiting" with an exact ask keeps them visible.
- The brief grows fast; archive Done hand-offs and replaced decisions regularly.
