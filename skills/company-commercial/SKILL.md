# Commercial & sales

## Source of truth

Read the Company Brief first (ID in the Project instructions; otherwise search Drive for "Company Brief"): retailer margin, tax case, price range and any fixed price, phase targets (for example sell-through and store count), approved market figures, who owns contracts, and open questions. Say which figures you used and whether each is a GM decision or a planning figure.

## Pitches

Structure: the customer's gap, the product, proof (only results that exist), the offer (terms below), the next step. Use only market figures approved in the brief, with sources; never a withdrawn figure; never a certification, shelf life or result the brief does not record as verified. Build decks in Google Slides in the company folder (or PowerPoint if the GM asks), 6 to 10 slides.

## Terms

For each customer, site or partner: price to them, their margin, the resulting shelf price, tax case, payment terms, firm sale or sale-or-return, minimum order, delivery frequency, display or equipment arrangements, exclusivity, length and exit. Before offering any term, run the price through the Finance method (use the company-finance skill if available). If a term pushes the price below Finance's floor or breaks a GM decision, do not offer it; send it to the GM with the numbers.

## Contracts

Keep a contract register as a Google Sheet, "Commercial – Contract register" (create once):

Counterparty | Type | Status (draft, sent, agreed, signed, ended) | Key terms | Value | Start | End | Notice | Finance checked | GM approved | Link

Draft contracts as Google Docs marked DRAFT. This is not legal advice: flag that a solicitor should review before signing, especially liability, product safety and termination. Nothing is signed without Finance's check and the GM's approval.

## Sales log

Keep "Commercial – Sales log" as a Google Sheet: date, site or customer, product, price, units delivered, sold, wasted or returned, sell-through % (formula). Never fill in sales that were not reported.

## Rules

- Never invent a buyer, store, site, fee, traffic or sales figure. Prospects found by research are marked "Not contacted".
- Do not change the Decisions log or Key assumptions; decisions go to the GM as open questions.

## Output

Lead with the recommendation, then the terms or pitch outline, the Finance check and what is unconfirmed. Finish with the Commercial & sales update (120 words or fewer: concluded, needs from other departments, open items, decisions for the GM).
